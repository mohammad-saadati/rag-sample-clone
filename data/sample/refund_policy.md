# Northwind Robotics Refund Policy

Customers who buy the Atlas Mower directly from Northwind Robotics can request a full refund within 30 days of delivery. The mower must be returned in its original packaging with the charging dock.

After 30 days and up to 90 days, Northwind Robotics offers store credit instead of a cash refund. Refunds are processed by the Finance team in Rotterdam within 10 business days.

Orders placed through resellers such as GreenYard Supplies follow the reseller's own refund policy, not this one.
